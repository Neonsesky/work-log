Connect your Google Sheet — one-time setup

Step 1 — Create your Google Sheet

Open sheets.google.com, create a new blank spreadsheet, and name it something like "Creative Work Log". Leave it open.

Step 2 — Add the Apps Script

In your Google Sheet, click Extensions → Apps Script. Delete anything in the editor and paste this entire script, then click Save (Ctrl+S):

Step 3 — Deploy as web app

1Click Deploy → New deployment
2Click the gear icon next to "Select type" and choose Web app
3Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone
4Click Deploy → authorize when prompted → copy the Web app URL

Step 4 — Paste your URL here

Paste the web app URL you just copied. It'll be saved in your browser and used every time you open this file.

Updating this app in the future

When you ask for a new version of this file with new features or fixes, just replace the HTML file — your Google Sheet data is completely separate and will never be affected. The URL you entered here is saved in your browser, so you won't need to re-enter it.
